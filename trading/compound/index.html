How to read this
  • Typical is the median: the middle result out of 300 simulated futures. Half do better, half do worse. The plain average would be higher, because a few huge lucky runs pull it up.
  • Bad luck is the 10th percentile: only 1 in 10 futures do worse.
  • Good luck is the 90th percentile: only 1 in 10 futures do better.
  • The shaded band covers 8 in 10 outcomes. Bigger monthly swings widen the band and drag the typical line down. That's volatility drag: −50% needs +100% just to get back to even.
